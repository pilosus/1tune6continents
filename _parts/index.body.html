1 TUNE and 6 CONTINENTS is a nonprofit collaborative media project. Leading musicians from six continents each interpret a single tune in their own creative style, and we edit their recordings into one shared track.

We offer a shared musical starting point, and the freedom to explore and make something new.

How a tune travels

Every project happens in two parts: first the professionals, then the next generation.

Part one: the lead musicians

Leading professional musicians from six continents interpret a given tune, each in their own creative style. They video-record themselves and we edit the footage into one creative, collaborative video track, posted on YouTube and social media.

Part two: the all-join-in medley

We send the track to musical and educational institutions in 195 countries, inviting young musicians from ABRSM Grade 5 to play their own version of the tune. We cut it together into one big medley to inspire the next generation of musicians and cultural leaders.

Creativity as a life skill

Cultural leaders are people who lead and inspire with creativity. Rather than creating pressure and expectations to achieve commercial goals, we are opening the way for young musicians and the next generation to be experimental, artistic and innovative.

Being creative is not just an activity to achieve a goal. It is an essential life skill that reduces social conflict and division around the world.

Help creativity travel further

This is a nonprofit project. We plan to approach cultural foundations in the hope of securing funding to pay the participants in the first part and the trainers in the second part.

We hope to inspire young people to become creative leaders: people who bring others together through creative ideas, not only perfect performances. Some may take the lead in future years.

If your foundation shares our belief in music, collaboration and young people’s creative leadership, we would welcome a conversation.
