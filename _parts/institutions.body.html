Music institutions

A place for inspiration and creativity

An invitation to musical and educational institutions.

In the second part of every project, we aim to send the tune to institutions in 195 countries, inviting young musicians from ABRSM Grade 5 to contribute their own versions of the tango to a large medley.

We hope educators will help young people explore creative choices, listen across cultures and discover the confidence to lead.
