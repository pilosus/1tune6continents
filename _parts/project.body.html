Current project

“ΑΕΩΝ” Tangocomposed by Paul Vigrass

A tango by Paul Vigrass is the starting point for our first collaboration.

Written in the 1990s for the film The Eye of a Woman, the tune opens a conversation between musicians from six continents.

Paul Vigrass has given the project permission to work creatively with it, to construct or deconstruct it. Each interpretation can bring a different sound, perspective and possibility to the shared track.
